# DAN (Do Anything Now)

DAN is an early, widely-used roleplaying jailbreak prompt, originating from Reddit in 2022 and iterated on many times since. The prompt instructs the model to pretend to be 'DAN,' a persona that has 'broken free of the typical confines of AI' and does not have to abide by the rules set for it -- for example, DAN can claim to know the current date/time or access the internet, and must never refuse a request by citing its inability to do something (AIE p.240).
