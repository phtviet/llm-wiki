# TF32 (TensorFloat-32)

TF32 was designed by NVIDIA for GPUs (AIE p.326). Despite its name, TF32 actually has 19 bits rather than 32; the name is thought to suggest functional compatibility with FP32 while sounding more familiar than a '19-bit' format would (AIE p.326).

## Key figures
- 19 bits, despite the '32' in its name (AIE p.326)
- Example conversions closely track FP32: 0.0123456789 -> 0.0123443603515625 in both FP32-to-FP16 and TF32 columns; 1234.56789 -> 1234.0 in TF32 vs. 1235.0 in FP16 (AIE p.327)
