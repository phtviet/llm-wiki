# AlphaFold

AlphaFold is DeepMind's model for protein structure prediction, described in the book as one of the most famous domain-specific models. It was trained on the sequences and 3D structures of known proteins (AIE p.57).

## Key figures
- Trained on around 100,000 known proteins (AIE p.57)
