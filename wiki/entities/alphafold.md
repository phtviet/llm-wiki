# AlphaFold

AlphaFold is DeepMind's domain-specific model for protein structure prediction, described in the book as one of the most famous domain-specific models (AIE p.57).

## Key figures
- Trained on the sequences and 3D structures of around 100,000 known proteins (AIE p.57)
