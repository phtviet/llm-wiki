# LAION

LAION is a non-profit organization that mobilized volunteer labelers worldwide to generate conversational data as a lower-cost alternative to high-quality paid human annotation (AIE p.83). Because the data came from self-selected volunteers rather than a controlled labeler pool, it carries demographic bias: in a self-reported survey, 90% of volunteer labelers identified as male (Köpf et al., 2023) (AIE p.83).

## Key figures
- Mobilized 13,500 volunteers, generating 10,000 conversations (161,443 messages) in 35 languages, annotated with 461,292 quality ratings (AIE p.83)
- 90% of self-reporting volunteer labelers identified as male (AIE p.83)
