# LAION

LAION is a non-profit organization that mobilized volunteers worldwide to generate demonstration data as a lower-cost alternative to hiring highly educated professional labelers. Because the data was generated by unpaid volunteers rather than curated labelers, there was little control for bias; in a self-reported survey, 90% of LAION's volunteer labelers identified as male (Köpf et al., 2023) (AIE p.82).

## Key figures
- Mobilized 13,500 volunteers worldwide to generate 10,000 conversations, consisting of 161,443 messages in 35 languages, annotated with 461,292 quality ratings (AIE p.82)
- 90% of volunteer labelers self-reported as male (AIE p.82)
