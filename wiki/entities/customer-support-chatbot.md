# Customer Support Chatbot (example)

The book uses a customer support chatbot as its running example for how to measure the business impact of an AI application. Relevant business metrics include what percentage of customer messages the chatbot should automate, how many more messages it allows the business to process, how much quicker it can respond compared to humans, and how much human labor it can save (AIE p.32). The book cautions that a chatbot answering more messages does not guarantee happier users, so customer satisfaction and feedback should also be tracked (AIE p.32).
