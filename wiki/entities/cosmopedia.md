# Cosmopedia

Cosmopedia (Allal et al., 2024) is a 25-billion-token synthetic [[pre-training|pre-training]] dataset of textbooks, blog posts, stories, posts, and WikiHow articles, generated by Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct-v0.1 (Jiang et al., 2024) (AIE p.387).

## Key figures
- 25 billion tokens (AIE p.387)
