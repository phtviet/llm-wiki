# MetaMath

MetaMath is a dataset created by Yu et al. (2023) by rewriting the 15,000 examples in MATH and GSM-8K in different ways, producing almost 400,000 examples. Models trained on MetaMath outperformed larger models on related math benchmarks (AIE p.387).

## Key figures
- Rewrote 15,000 source examples into almost 400,000 examples (AIE p.387)
