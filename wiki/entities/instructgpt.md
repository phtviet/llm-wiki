# InstructGPT

InstructGPT is OpenAI's text-only model finetuned on demonstration data to follow instructions rather than merely complete text. The book uses its published training distribution as an example of the range of task types (question answering, summarization, translation, and more) that demonstration data must cover; this distribution does not contain multimodal tasks, since InstructGPT is text-only (AIE p.81).

Among the labelers who created InstructGPT's demonstration data, about 90% had at least a college degree and more than one-third had a master's degree (AIE p.82).

## Key figures
- ~90% of demonstration-data labelers had at least a college degree; more than one-third had a master's degree (AIE p.82)
- OpenAI used 13,000 (prompt, response) demonstration pairs to finetune InstructGPT, estimated at $130,000 at $10/pair (AIE p.82)
