# INT8 and INT4

INT8 (8-bit integers) and INT4 (4-bit integers) are common integer numerical formats, also called fixed point formats. Integer representations are not yet as common as floating point formats in AI workloads, but are becoming increasingly popular (AIE p.326).

## Key figures
- INT8: 8-bit integer format (AIE p.326)
- INT4: 4-bit integer format (AIE p.326)
