# Goliath-120B

Goliath-120B (alpindale, 2023) is an early success case of frankenmerging (layer stacking). It was merged from two finetuned Llama 2-70B models, Xwin and Euryale, taking 72 out of 80 layers from each and merging them together (AIE p.354).
