# Google Gemini

Google Gemini is a conversational AI product that, unlike some competitors, shows only the beginning of each candidate response when presenting side-by-side comparative choices to users. Users must click to expand a response before reading it fully, which itself becomes a feedback signal about which response the user found more promising (AIE p.483).
