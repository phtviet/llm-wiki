# LinkedIn

LinkedIn is cited (2024) as a real-world example of the [[milestone-planning]] last-mile challenge: it took the company one month to achieve 80% of the AI product experience they wanted, an early success that made them grossly underestimate how long it would take to improve the product further (AIE p.33).
