# GPT Instruction-Tuning Comparison Study (Peng et al., 2023)

A study by a group of Microsoft researchers, published as "Instruction Tuning with GPT-4" (Peng et al., 2023), that compared GPT-3's and GPT-4's generations for the same set of instructions. The researchers used the distribution of (verb, direct object, noun) pairs and response length as statistics to characterize the difference between the two models' outputs, demonstrating that this kind of distributional analysis is useful for evaluating data and, by extension, for evaluating models (AIE p.397).

The study found that GPT-4 has a broader and more diverse range of verb-noun pairings than GPT-3 and tends to generate longer responses (AIE p.397).

## Key figures
None. The reported findings are qualitative (broader/more diverse pairings, longer responses), not load-bearing numeric figures.
