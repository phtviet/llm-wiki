# MIT ChatGPT Writing Study

A study by Noy and Zhang (2023) measuring ChatGPT's impact on occupation-specific writing tasks. Researchers assigned such tasks to 453 college-educated professionals and randomly exposed half of them to ChatGPT (AIE p.23).

The study found ChatGPT closes the output-quality gap between workers, meaning it helps less-inclined writers more than skilled ones. Adoption also persisted after the experiment ended (AIE p.23).

## Key figures
- 453 college-educated professionals participated, half randomly exposed to ChatGPT (AIE p.23)
- Average time taken decreased by 40%, output quality rose by 18%, among those exposed to ChatGPT (AIE p.23)
- Exposed workers were 2 times as likely to report using ChatGPT in their real job two weeks after the experiment, and 1.6 times as likely two months after that (AIE p.23)
