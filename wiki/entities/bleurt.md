# BLEURT

BLEURT (Sellam et al., 2020) is a reference-based judge that takes a (candidate response, reference response) pair and outputs a similarity score between them (AIE p.147).

## Key figures
- Score range is approximately -2.5 to 1.0, an arbitrary range that illustrates the criteria-ambiguity challenge with AI judges (AIE p.146)
