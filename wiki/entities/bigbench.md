# BIG-bench

BIG-bench is Google's 2022 collection of benchmarks for evaluating language models, illustrating the sheer scale of the public-benchmark landscape: as more AI use cases emerge and old benchmarks saturate, new benchmarks proliferate rapidly (AIE p.191).

## Key figures
- Contains 214 benchmarks (AIE p.191)
