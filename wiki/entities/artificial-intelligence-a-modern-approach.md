# Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach

A textbook by Stuart Russell and Peter Norvig (Prentice Hall, 1995) that defines the field of artificial intelligence research as 'the study and design of rational agents.' It defines an agent as anything that can be viewed as perceiving its environment through sensors and acting upon that environment through actuators (AIE p.275).
