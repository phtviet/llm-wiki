# Alpaca

Alpaca is a model produced by finetuning Llama-7B, the 7-billion-parameter version of Llama, on examples generated by text-davinci-003, a 175-billion-parameter teacher model (Taori et al., 2023). It is the book's example of a student distilled by **finetuning a pre-trained model**, as distinct from training from scratch (the DistilBERT route). The resulting model behaves similarly to text-davinci-003 despite being a small fraction of its size (AIE p.395).

## Key figures
- Llama-7B (student) finetuned on text-davinci-003 (175B-parameter teacher) outputs (AIE p.395)
- Alpaca is 4% the size of its teacher model, text-davinci-003 (AIE p.395)
