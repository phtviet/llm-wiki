# Alpaca

Alpaca is a model distilled by finetuning, produced in 2023 by Taori et al. finetuning
Llama-7B (the 7-billion-parameter version of Llama) on examples generated by
text-davinci-003, a 175-billion-parameter teacher model. The resulting model behaves
similarly to text-davinci-003 despite its much smaller size (AIE p.395). It is the
book's example of a student **finetuned from a pre-trained model**, as distinct from
the trained-from-scratch route ([[distilbert]]). See [[model-distillation]] for the
general method.

## Key figures
- Llama-7B (7-billion-parameter student) finetuned on text-davinci-003 (175-billion-parameter teacher) outputs (AIE p.395)
- Alpaca is 4% the size of the teacher model (AIE p.395)
