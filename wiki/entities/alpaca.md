# Alpaca

Alpaca is a student model produced by finetuning Llama-7B, the 7-billion-parameter
version of Llama, on examples generated by text-davinci-003, a 175-billion-parameter
model (Taori et al., 2023). It is the book's example of a student **finetuned from a
pre-trained model**, as distinct from training from scratch (the DistilBERT route). The
resulting model behaves similarly to text-davinci-003 despite its much smaller size,
illustrating [[model-distillation]] via finetuning (AIE p.395).

## Key figures
- Llama-7B (7-billion-parameter base) finetuned on outputs from text-davinci-003 (175-billion-parameter teacher) (AIE p.395)
- Resulting Alpaca model is 4% the size of the teacher model, while behaving similarly to it (AIE p.395)
