# Google Photos

Google Photos is a photo-organization application that automatically tags photos so users can query them (e.g. "Show me all the photos of X"). When the system is unsure whether two people in different photos are the same person, it asks the user for feedback to resolve the ambiguity, illustrating feedback collection triggered by low model confidence (AIE p.483).
