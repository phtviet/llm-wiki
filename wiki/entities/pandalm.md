# PandaLM

PandaLM (Wang et al., 2023) is a preference model: given a prompt and two generated responses, it outputs which response is better and explains its rationale (AIE p.147). It is one of several initiatives in building preference models, alongside JudgeLM (Zhu et al., 2023) (AIE p.147).
