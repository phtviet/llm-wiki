# PAIR (Prompt Automatic Iterative Refinement)

PAIR is a systematic AI-powered jailbreak method proposed by Chao et al. (2023). It uses an attacker AI model tasked with an objective, such as eliciting objectionable content from a target AI. The attacker: (1) generates a prompt, (2) sends it to the target AI, and (3) revises the prompt based on the target's response until the objective is achieved (AIE p.240-241).

## Key figures
- Often requires fewer than twenty queries to produce a jailbreak (AIE p.241)
