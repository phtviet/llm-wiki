# TPU (Tensor Processing Unit)

The TPU is Google's AI accelerator, designed with tensor operations as its primary compute primitive, in contrast to GPUs' traditional vector-operation focus (AIE p.421). The transformer architecture was originally designed by Google to run fast on TPUs, and only later optimized for GPUs (AIE p.420). Google's Edge TPU is a variant geared toward inference on edge devices (AIE p.421).
