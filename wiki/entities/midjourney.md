# Midjourney

Midjourney is an AI image-generation startup, cited in the book as an example of AI's strength at creative, probabilistic tasks. It reached $200 million in annual recurring revenue at just one and a half years old, illustrating the speed of commercial traction for creative AI applications (AIE p.22).

## Key figures
- $200 million in annual recurring revenue at one and a half years old (as of late 2023) (AIE p.22)
