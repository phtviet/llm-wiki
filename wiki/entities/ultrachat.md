# UltraChat

UltraChat is a multi-turn dialogue dataset synthesized by Ding et al. (2023). The authors asked ChatGPT to generate 30 topics spanning aspects of daily life (technology, food and drink, fashion, nature, education, finance, travel, etc.), then asked ChatGPT to generate 30 to 50 subtopics per topic. The same model then generated instructions and corresponding responses for these subtopics (AIE p.389).
