# BOLD

BOLD (Bias in Open-ended Language Generation Dataset) (Dhamala et al., 2021) is a benchmark used to measure bias in open-ended text generation, alongside RealToxicityPrompts as a common toxicity/bias benchmark (AIE p.171).
