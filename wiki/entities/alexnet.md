# AlexNet

AlexNet (Krizhevsky et al., 2012) is the model that started the deep learning revolution. It was a supervised model trained to classify over 1 million images from the ImageNet dataset into one of 1,000 categories such as 'car', 'balloon', or 'monkey' (AIE p.6).

## Key figures
- Trained on over 1 million labeled ImageNet images across 1,000 categories (AIE p.6)
