# Chegg

Chegg is a company that helps students with their homework. The section uses it as an
example of an education business disrupted by the rise of generative AI, as students
turned to AI tools like ChatGPT for help instead (AIE p.25).

## Key figures
- Share price fell from $28 when ChatGPT launched in November 2022 to $2 by September 2024 (AIE p.25)
