# RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)

RLHF is a technique for [[preference-finetuning]] that uses reinforcement learning to
align a model's outputs with human preference. The book names it as used by GPT-3.5 and
Llama 2 (AIE p.78). It is contrasted with DPO (Direct Preference Optimization), used by
Llama 3, and RLAIF (reinforcement learning from AI feedback), potentially used by Claude
(AIE p.78). The book's Figure 2-10 depicts the overall training workflow of
pre-training, SFT, and RLHF as the assumed final step (AIE p.79).
