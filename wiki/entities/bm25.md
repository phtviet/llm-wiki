# BM25 (Okapi BM25)

Okapi BM25, the 25th generation of the Best Matching algorithm, was developed by Robertson et al. in the 1980s. Its scorer is a modification of TF-IDF: compared to naive [[tf-idf]], BM25 normalizes term frequency scores by document length, since longer documents are more likely to contain a given term and have higher term-frequency values (AIE p.259). BM25 and its variants (BM25+, BM25F) remain widely used in industry as formidable baselines against which modern retrieval algorithms, such as embedding-based retrieval, are compared (AIE p.259).
