# Chip Huyen

Chip Huyen is a writer and computer scientist specializing in machine learning (ML) systems. She has worked at NVIDIA and Snorkel AI, founded an AI infrastructure startup that was later acquired, and taught ML systems at Stanford University. This book, *AI Engineering*, draws on her experience helping major organizations and startups apply AI to practical solutions (AIE p.510).

She previously wrote *Designing Machine Learning Systems* (O'Reilly, 2022), an Amazon bestseller in AI translated into over 10 languages. She is also the author of four bestselling Vietnamese books, including the series *Xach ba lo len va Di* (*Pack Your Bag and Go*) (AIE p.510).
