# BF16 (BFloat16)

BF16 was designed by Google to optimize AI performance on TPUs (AIE p.326). It uses the same number of bits as FP16, but allocates more bits to range and fewer to precision, letting it represent larger values that would be out-of-bound for FP16, at the cost of being less precise overall (AIE p.327). Llama 2 shipped with its weights in BF16; teams that instead loaded the model in FP16 found its quality much worse than advertised, since loading a model in the wrong numerical format can cause it to change significantly (AIE p.328).

## Key figures
- Same bit width as FP16 (16 bits) but more range bits, fewer precision bits (AIE p.327)
- 1234.56789 converts to 1232.0 in BF16, a 0.208% value change, versus 1235.0 (0.035%) in FP16 (AIE p.327)
- 123456.789, out of bound in FP16, converts to 123392.0 in BF16 (AIE p.327)
