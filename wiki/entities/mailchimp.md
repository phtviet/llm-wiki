# Mailchimp

Mailchimp is cited as an example of a company whose original product could have been
built instead as a feature of a larger, existing product — in this case, a feature of
Gmail. It illustrates how startups can succeed by building on top of a gap big
incumbents overlooked (AIE p.32).
