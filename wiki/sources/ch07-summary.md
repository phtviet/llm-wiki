# AIE -- AI Engineering (Chip Huyen, O'Reilly, 2024)

Immutable source. Citation label **AIE**. Cite as (AIE p.NNN) using printed book pages.
Covered by this ingest: Chapter 7 summary (Finetuning), book pp.361-363.

This section recaps the finetuning chapter: the choice of whether to finetune at all,
the shift from full finetuning to memory-efficient approaches like PEFT and quantized
training, LoRA's properties, model merging as an extension of combining finetuned
models, and a forward pointer to the next chapter's treatment of data challenges for
finetuning. It also notes, via a footnote, the growth in the number of people credited
with data work between GPT-3 (two people) and GPT-4 (eighty people), citing OpenAI's
2020 and 2023 papers and a 2016 AMA comment from OpenAI cofounder Wojciech Zaremba
about using publicly available datasets (AIE p.362).
