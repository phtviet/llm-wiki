# AIE — AI Engineering (Chip Huyen, O'Reilly, 2024)

Immutable source. Citation label **AIE**. Cite as (AIE p.NNN) using printed book pages.
Covered by this ingest: Chapter 8 summary, 'Dataset Engineering,' book pp.403-405.

This section recaps the chapter's themes: dataset design starts from the behaviors a
team wants a model to learn; data needs differ by training phase (pre-training,
instruction finetuning, preference finetuning) but share three criteria -- quality,
coverage, and quantity (AIE p.403). It reiterates that small high-quality datasets can
outperform large noisy ones and that dataset diversity is key to performance, notes the
rise of dedicated data roles for acquisition, privacy, and compliance, and restates that
synthetic data only became broadly practical once AI could generate realistic, complex
data (AIE p.403). It closes by noting synthetic data still requires quality evaluation,
that many dataset-creation steps (annotation, annotation guidelines, verification) resist
automation, and previews the next chapter's topic of inference optimization for latency
and cost (AIE p.404-405).
