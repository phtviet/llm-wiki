# AIE — AI Engineering (Chip Huyen, O'Reilly, 2024)

Covered by this ingest: Chapter 5, 'Break Complex Tasks into Simpler Subtasks', book pp.224-227. Covers decomposing complex prompting tasks into chained subtask prompts, illustrated with a customer support intent-classification and response-generation example, the benefits (monitoring, debugging, parallelization, effort) and downsides (latency, cost) of decomposition, and GoDaddy's experience shrinking a bloated 1,500-token prompt by decomposing it.
