# AIE -- AI Engineering (Chip Huyen, O'Reilly, 2024)

This section discusses AI's use in education: personalized learning materials, language-learning roleplay, quiz generation and grading, AI debate partners, and AI teaching assistants (e.g., Khan Academy). It notes Duolingo's finding that AI benefits course-creation stages unevenly, with lesson personalization benefiting most. It also covers AI's disruptive effect on education businesses, citing Chegg's stock decline after ChatGPT's launch, and frames AI's dual role as both a threat to certain skills and an opportunity for on-demand tutoring.
