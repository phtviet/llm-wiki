# AIE -- AI Engineering (Chip Huyen, O'Reilly, 2024)

Covered by this ingest: Chapter 3, 'Understanding Language Modeling Metrics', book pp.118-119. Notes that foundation models still rely on language models as a core component, that a language model's performance tends to correlate with downstream foundation-model performance (Liu et al., 2023), and that language modeling metrics -- cross entropy, perplexity, bits-per-character, and bits-per-byte -- have changed little since Claude Shannon's 1951 paper 'Prediction and Entropy of Printed English'.
