throughput, utilization), the TTFT/TPOT latency split, hardware dependence, and the
model-level versus inference-service-level split of optimization techniques. It
restates that quantization and distillation are model-agnostic techniques, attention

parallelism, prefill/decode decoupling, and prompt caching are service-level
