# Conversational Bots

Conversational bots are AI systems that help users find information, explain concepts,
brainstorm ideas, and act as companions or therapists, including emulating specific
personalities. For enterprises, the most common form is the customer support bot, which
can cut costs while improving customer experience by responding to users faster than
human agents. AI can also serve as a product copilot, guiding customers through
confusing tasks such as filing insurance claims, doing taxes, or looking up corporate
policies (AIE p.26).

Text is not the only interface: voice assistants (e.g. Google Assistant, Siri, Alexa)
have existed for years, and 3D conversational bots are already common in games and
gaining traction in retail and marketing. One use case is smart NPCs (non-player
characters) in games, which AI can make far more capable than traditional scripted
characters with a limited range of dialogue, potentially changing the dynamics of
existing games and enabling new ones (AIE p.26).

Researchers have also used groups of conversational bots to simulate a society, enabling
studies of social dynamics (Park et al., 2023) (AIE p.26).
