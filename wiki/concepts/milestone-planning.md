# Milestone Planning

Milestone planning is the process of laying out how to reach an AI product's measurable goals once those goals are set. The starting plan depends on evaluating existing off-the-shelf models first: the stronger a model's out-of-the-box capabilities, the less additional work is needed to hit the target. For example, if the goal is automating 60% of customer support tickets and an off-the-shelf model already automates 30%, the remaining effort is smaller than if it automates none (AIE p.33). Evaluation results often force a revision of goals -- a team may find the resources needed to reach a [[usefulness-threshold]] exceed the potential return, making the project not worth pursuing (AIE p.33).

Planning must also account for the [[last-mile-challenge]]: an impressive initial demo, easy to build given how capable foundation models already are, does not promise a good end product, since a demo built in a weekend can take months or years to mature into a product (AIE p.33).
