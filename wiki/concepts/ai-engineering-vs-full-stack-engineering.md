# AI Engineering Versus Full-Stack Engineering

The rising importance of AI interfaces pushes AI engineering closer to full-stack
development. As application development gains emphasis, especially around interfaces,
AI tooling design shifts to attract more frontend engineers, a change from traditional
ML engineering's Python-centric focus, where the most popular ML frameworks supported
mostly Python APIs (AIE p.46).

The book summarizes this shift by comparing the importance of categories in app
development for traditional ML versus foundation models: AI interface goes from less
important to important, prompt engineering goes from not applicable to important, and
evaluation goes from important to more important (AIE p.46).

New AI interfaces (standalone apps, browser extensions, chatbots integrated into chat
apps, plug-in APIs, voice-based, and embodied interfaces) also change how user feedback
is collected: conversational interfaces make it easier for users to give feedback in
natural language, but that feedback is harder to extract (AIE p.46).
