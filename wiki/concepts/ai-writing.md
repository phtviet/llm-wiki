# AI Writing

AI writing assistance is a foundation-model use case covering autocomplete, drafting, tone-editing, and full-document generation. Writing suits AI well because it is frequent, tedious, and tolerant of mistakes: a bad suggestion can simply be ignored (AIE p.22). LLMs are naturally good at writing since they are trained for text completion (AIE p.23).

For consumers, uses include rewriting emails for tone, turning bullet points into paragraphs, essay writing, and AI-generated fiction (including interactive, reader-influenced plots and children's stories targeted at a child's vocabulary gaps). Note-taking and email apps (Google Docs, Notion, Gmail) build AI into writing help, and [[grammarly]] finetunes a model for fluency and clarity (AIE p.23).

For enterprises, AI writing supports sales, marketing, team communication, performance reports, cold outreach, ad copywriting, and product descriptions; CRM tools like HubSpot and Salesforce include generation features for web content and outreach (AIE p.23).

The same capability is abused: AI-generated travel guidebooks flooded Amazon with fabricated author bios and reviews, and AI's strength at SEO (likely from training on SEO-optimized internet text) has enabled content farms that mass-produce junk articles to sell ad space (AIE p.23-24).
