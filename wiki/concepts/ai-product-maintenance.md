# AI Product Maintenance

Maintaining an AI product does not stop once launch goals are met: teams must plan
for how the product will change over time, a challenge sharpened by the fast pace of
change in the AI field (AIE p.34). Many of these changes are favorable to maintainers:
model context lengths are lengthening, output quality is improving, and [[inference-optimization|model inference]] is getting faster and cheaper, so a product built on
foundation models is committing to keep riding this fast-moving wave rather than
standing still (AIE p.34).

## Key figures
None. The section illustrates the trend (inference cost dropping, MMLU performance
rising between 2022 and 2024) without giving a specific load-bearing figure of its own (AIE p.34).
