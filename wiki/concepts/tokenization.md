# Tokenization

Tokenization is the process of breaking original text into [[token]]s (AIE p.3). The tokenization method and the resulting vocabulary size are decisions made by model developers, and they vary between models -- for example, Mixtral 8x7B and GPT-4 tokenize text differently and end up with very different vocabulary sizes (AIE p.3).

## Key figures
None. Vocabulary-size figures are model-specific and recorded under [[vocabulary]] / on the relevant model pages.
