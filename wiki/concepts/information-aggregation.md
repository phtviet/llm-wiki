# Information Aggregation

Information aggregation is the use of AI to filter, summarize, and distill useful
information from large volumes of content -- emails, Slack messages, news, documents,
meeting notes -- so people are not overwhelmed by keeping up with it (AIE p.26). It
pairs closely with summarization and is presented as one of the earliest
production-proven uses of generative AI.

For consumers, applications can process documents such as contracts, disclosures, and
papers and let users retrieve information conversationally, a use case called
talk-to-your-docs; AI can also summarize websites and research and create reports on
chosen topics (AIE p.26). For enterprises, information aggregation and dissemination
can make an organization leaner by reducing the burden on middle management, and can
surface critical information about customers or competitors (AIE p.27). An example is
Instacart's internal prompt marketplace, whose most popular template, "Fast
Breakdown," summarizes meeting notes, emails, and Slack conversations into facts, open
questions, and action items that can then be fed into a project tracking tool and
assigned to owners (AIE p.27).

## Key figures
- 74% of generative AI users use it to distill complex ideas and summarize information, per Salesforce's 2023 Generative AI Snapshot Research (AIE p.26)
