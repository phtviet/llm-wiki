# Information Aggregation

Information aggregation is the use of AI to filter, digest, and summarize large volumes
of incoming information -- emails, Slack messages, news -- that can otherwise overwhelm
users. AI's capability to aggregate and summarize information is presented as a direct
answer to this overload problem (AIE p.26).

## Key figures
- 74% of generative AI users use it to distill complex ideas and summarize information, per Salesforce's 2023 Generative AI Snapshot Research (AIE p.26)
