# Model Development

Model development is the layer of the AI engineering stack most commonly associated with traditional ML engineering. It has three main responsibilities: [[model-adaptation|modeling and training]], [[dataset-engineering]], and [[inference-optimization]]; evaluation also belongs here but is discussed as part of application development instead, since most people encounter it there first (AIE p.39-40).

With foundation models available, deep ML knowledge (gradient descent, loss functions, regularization, neural architectures) is no longer a strict prerequisite for building AI applications, though it remains valuable for expanding one's toolset and for troubleshooting (AIE p.41).
