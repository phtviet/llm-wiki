# Pointwise Evaluation

Pointwise evaluation assesses each model independently, giving it its own score, and then ranks models by comparing those scores. This contrasts with [[comparative-evaluation]], which evaluates models against each other directly and derives a ranking from comparison outcomes rather than from independent scores (AIE p.148).

For responses whose quality is subjective, comparative evaluation is typically easier to carry out than pointwise evaluation: it is easier to judge which of two songs is better than to assign each song a concrete score (AIE p.149).
