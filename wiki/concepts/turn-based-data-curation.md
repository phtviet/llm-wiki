# Single-Turn vs. Multi-Turn Data

When curating data for conversational applications, a key decision is whether to collect single-turn data, multi-turn data, or both. Single-turn data trains a model to respond to individual instructions and is simpler and easier to obtain. Multi-turn data teaches a model how to solve tasks that involve back-and-forth exchanges -- for instance, a model may need to first clarify a user's intent before addressing a task, and the user may then provide corrections or additional information for the next step. Multi-turn data typically requires purpose-built scenarios or more involved interactions to capture, making it harder to obtain than single-turn data (AIE p.366-367).
