# AI in Education

Education is a foundation-model use case in which AI helps students learn rather than
just completing tasks for them. Instead of schools banning tools like ChatGPT over
cheating concerns, the section argues AI can be incorporated to personalize learning:
summarizing textbooks, generating personalized lecture plans, and adapting material
format to the individual student -- reading materials aloud for auditory learners,
adding animal-themed visualizations, or translating math equations into code (AIE
p.24).

Language learning benefits especially, since AI can roleplay practice scenarios.
Citing Pajak and Bicknell (Duolingo, 2022), the section notes that of the four stages
of course creation, lesson personalization benefits most from AI. AI can also generate
and evaluate quizzes, and serve as a debate partner, presenting multiple views on a
topic better than the average human. Khan Academy offers AI-powered teaching
assistants to students and course assistants to teachers, and some teachers assign
AI-generated essays for students to find and correct mistakes (AIE p.25).

The section frames a risk/opportunity tradeoff: AI threatens companies whose business
is helping students with tasks AI can now do, but the same capability lets AI act as a
tutor for any skill, helping someone get up to speed quickly before continuing to learn
independently (AIE p.25).
