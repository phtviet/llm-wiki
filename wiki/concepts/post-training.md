# Post-training

Post-training is the process of training a model after the [[pre-training]] phase; conceptually it is the same as [[finetuning]] and the terms are often used interchangeably. Where a distinction is drawn, it's usually about who performs the work and why: post-training is typically done by model developers (e.g., OpenAI post-training a model to better follow instructions before release), while finetuning is typically done by application developers adapting an already-released, possibly post-trained, model to their own needs (AIE p.42). Pre-training and post-training make up a spectrum, sharing similar processes and toolings (AIE p.42).
