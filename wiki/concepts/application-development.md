# Application Development

Application development is the AI engineering stack layer where, since many teams now build on the same shared foundation models, differentiation must come from the application layer rather than from proprietary model quality (AIE p.44). It consists of three responsibilities: [[evaluation]], [[prompt-engineering]], and [[ai-interface]] (AIE p.44).
