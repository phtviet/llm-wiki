# MTEB (Massive Text Embedding Benchmark)

MTEB is a benchmark that measures embedding quality across multiple tasks (Muennighoff et al., 2023), given as the book's example of how embedding quality can be evaluated based on utility for downstream tasks such as classification, topic modeling, recommender systems, and RAG (AIE p.135).
