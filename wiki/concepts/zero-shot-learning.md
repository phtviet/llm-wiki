# Zero-Shot Learning

Zero-shot learning is [[in-context-learning]] performed with no examples (shots) given in the prompt, in contrast to [[few-shot-learning]]. Microsoft's 2023 analysis found that on GPT-4 and other more powerful models, few-shot learning produced only limited improvement over zero-shot learning for the use cases studied, suggesting stronger models need fewer or no examples to follow instructions well (AIE p.214).
