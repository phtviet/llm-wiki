# Compound Mistakes

Compound mistakes is one of two reasons agents typically require more powerful models than non-agent use cases (the other being higher stakes). An agent often needs to perform multiple steps to accomplish a task, and overall accuracy decreases as the number of steps increases (AIE p.277).

## Key figures
- At 95% accuracy per step, accuracy falls to 60% after 10 steps and to 0.6% after 100 steps (AIE p.277)
