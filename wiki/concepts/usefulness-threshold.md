# Usefulness Threshold

A usefulness threshold is a clear expectation of how good an AI product has to be before it is useful enough to put in front of customers. Setting this threshold before launch ensures a product isn't shipped prematurely (AIE p.32). The book groups usefulness-threshold metrics into: quality metrics (measuring response quality), latency metrics (including [[time-to-first-token]], time per output token, and total latency), cost metrics (cost per inference request), and other metrics such as interpretability and fairness (AIE p.33).

Acceptable latency is use-case dependent: if customer requests are currently handled by humans with a median response time of an hour, any chatbot response faster than that may already be good enough (AIE p.33).
