# AI Product Defensibility

AI product defensibility is the question of what moat protects a standalone AI product from competitors, given that the low barrier to building on foundation models is both a blessing and a curse: anything easy for you to build is also easy for a competitor to build (AIE p.31). Building an application on top of a foundation model means providing a layer on top of that model; if the underlying model's capabilities expand, that layer risks being subsumed, rendering the application obsolete (AIE p.31). The book's example: a PDF-parsing application built on the assumption that the underlying model can't parse PDFs well loses its rationale for competing once that assumption stops holding, though the application might still make sense if it's built on top of something else the model doesn't subsume (AIE p.31).
