# Model as a Service

Model as a service is the practice of a small number of well-resourced organizations developing large models and making them available for others to use as a service, rather than requiring every team to train its own (AIE p.1). Because training large language models demands data, compute, and specialized talent that only a few organizations can afford, this arrangement lets anyone build AI applications by using existing models without investing upfront in building one (AIE p.1). This lowered barrier to entry, combined with rising demand for AI applications, is presented as a key driver behind the growth of [[ai-engineering]] (AIE p.1).
