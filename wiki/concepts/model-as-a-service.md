# Model as a Service

Model as a service is an arrangement in which a small number of well-resourced organizations develop large models and make them available for others to use as a service, rather than requiring every team to build a model from scratch. Training large language models requires data, compute, and specialized talent that only a few organizations can afford; making those models available as a service lets anyone leverage AI to build applications without investing up front in building a model themselves (AIE p.1).

This dynamic is one of the two forces the book identifies behind the growth of [[ai-engineering]]: demand for AI applications has increased while the barrier to entry for building them has decreased (AIE p.1).
