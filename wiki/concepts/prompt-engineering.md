# Prompt Engineering

Prompt engineering is the technique of crafting detailed instructions, including examples of desirable outputs, to get a foundation model to generate what you want without changing its weights (AIE p.11). The book's example: to get a general-purpose model to generate on-brand product descriptions for a retailer, one can craft detailed instructions with examples of the desired output rather than relying on the model's out-of-the-box behavior (AIE p.10-11). Prompt engineering, [[retrieval-augmented-generation]], and [[finetuning]] are presented as three common techniques for adapting a foundation model to specific needs (AIE p.11).
