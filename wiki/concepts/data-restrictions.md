# Data Restrictions

As companies recognize the value of their data for AI training, many -- including Reddit and Stack Overflow -- have changed their data terms to prevent scraping for model training (AIE p.76). Longpre et al. (2024) found that between 2023 and 2024, a rapid increase in data restrictions from web sources left over 28% of the most critical sources in the C4 dataset fully restricted, and changes to Terms of Service and crawling rules now restrict 45% of C4 overall (AIE p.76-77). This dynamic pushes the most feasible path for more human-generated training data toward proprietary data -- unique sources like copyrighted books, translations, contracts, medical records, and genome sequences -- which the book frames as a coming competitive advantage in the AI race; it is why OpenAI negotiated data deals with publishers such as Axel Springer and the Associated Press (AIE p.76).

## Key figures
- Over 28% of C4's most critical sources fully restricted between 2023 and 2024; 45% of C4 restricted overall (AIE p.76-77)
