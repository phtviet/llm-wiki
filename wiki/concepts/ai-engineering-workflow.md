# AI Engineering Workflow

The new AI engineering workflow inverts the traditional ML engineering order. In
traditional ML engineering, work starts with gathering data and training a model, and
building the product comes last, with model development and product development
often disjointed and ML engineers rarely involved in product decisions. With
foundation models readily available, it becomes possible to start with building the
product first, and only invest in data and models once the product shows promise
(AIE p.46). This rewards teams and individuals who can iterate fast, and AI
engineers tend to be much more involved in building the product than traditional ML
engineers were (AIE p.47).
