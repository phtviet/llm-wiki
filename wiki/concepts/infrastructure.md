# Infrastructure

Infrastructure is the bottom layer of the [[ai-stack]]. It includes tooling for model serving, managing data and compute, and monitoring (AIE p.37). Unlike the application-development and model-development layers, infrastructure grew comparatively little during the 2023 surge in AI tooling, since core infrastructural needs -- resource management, serving, monitoring -- remain largely the same even as the models and applications built on top of them change (AIE p.38).
