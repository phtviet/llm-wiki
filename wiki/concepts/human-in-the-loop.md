# Human-in-the-Loop

Human-in-the-loop refers to involving humans in an AI system's decision-making process, rather than letting AI act fully autonomously (AIE p.30). For a customer support chatbot, this can take several forms: AI shows candidate responses that human agents reference to write faster replies, AI answers only simple requests and routes complex ones to humans, or AI answers all requests directly without human involvement (AIE p.30).

Microsoft (2023) proposed a Crawl-Walk-Run framework for gradually increasing AI automation: Crawl means human involvement is mandatory; Walk means AI can directly interact with internal employees; Run means increased automation, potentially including direct AI interaction with external users (AIE p.30). The appropriate level of human involvement can shift over time as AI system quality improves -- for example, starting with AI suggestions for human agents, then, once acceptance of those suggestions is high, letting AI respond directly for simple requests (AIE p.30).
