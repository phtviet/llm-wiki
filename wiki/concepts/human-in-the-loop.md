# Human-in-the-Loop

Human-in-the-loop refers to involving humans in AI's decision-making process rather than letting AI act fully autonomously (AIE p.31). For a customer support chatbot, this can take several forms: AI shows several candidate responses that human agents reference to write faster replies, AI answers only simple requests and routes complex ones to humans, or AI responds to all requests directly without human involvement (AIE p.31).

Microsoft (2023) proposed a Crawl-Walk-Run framework for gradually increasing AI automation: Crawl means human involvement is mandatory; Walk means AI can interact directly with internal employees; Run means increased automation, potentially including direct AI interaction with external users (AIE p.31). The role of humans can shift over time as AI quality improves — for example, starting by generating suggestions for human agents, then, if human acceptance of AI-suggested responses is high (e.g., 95% used verbatim for simple requests), letting AI interact with customers directly for those requests (AIE p.31).

## Key figures
- Example acceptance-rate threshold: 95% of AI-suggested responses to simple requests used verbatim by human agents as a signal to allow direct AI-customer interaction (AIE p.31)
