# Benchmark Saturation

A benchmark becomes saturated for a model once that model achieves the perfect (or near-perfect) score on it, at which point the benchmark stops being useful for measuring further progress. With foundation models, benchmarks are becoming saturated increasingly fast, necessitating a steady stream of replacement benchmarks (AIE p.115).

## Key figures
- GLUE (2018) saturated within a year, prompting SuperGLUE (2019) (AIE p.115-116)
- NaturalInstructions (2021) was replaced by SuperNaturalInstructions (2022) (AIE p.116)
- MMLU (2020) was largely replaced by MMLU-Pro (2024) (AIE p.116)
