# Success Metrics for AI Applications

After deciding to build an AI application, the next step is defining what success looks
like by measuring impact on the business. The most important metric is business
impact, not model accuracy in isolation. For a customer support chatbot example, this
includes: what percentage of customer messages the chatbot should automate, how
many more messages it should allow the business to process, how much quicker
responses become, and how much human labor it saves (AIE p.32).
